# Project management fundamentals

> Starter notes. Replace this file with your own lecture notes for EEU 08106.

A **project** is a temporary effort with a defined start and end, undertaken to create a unique product, service or result. **Project management** is applying knowledge, skills, tools and techniques to meet the project's requirements.

## The triple constraint

Every project balances **scope**, **time** and **cost**, with **quality** at the centre. Changing one affects the others: a bigger scope needs more time or more money.

## Project life cycle

1. **Initiation**: feasibility study, project charter, stakeholders identified.
2. **Planning**: scope (WBS), schedule, budget, risks, communication plan.
3. **Execution**: the team does the work and resources are managed.
4. **Monitoring and control**: progress is tracked against the baseline and changes are controlled.
5. **Closure**: handover, final report, lessons learned.

## Work breakdown structure (WBS)

A WBS splits the total scope into smaller, manageable **work packages**. The **100% rule**: the WBS must include all the work in the project and nothing outside it.

## Stakeholders

Anyone who affects or is affected by the project: client, sponsor, users, contractors, regulators and the community. Map them by **power and interest** to decide how closely to manage each group.

## Risk management

$$\text{Risk exposure} = \text{Probability} \times \text{Impact}$$

Responses to a threat: **avoid**, **transfer** (such as insurance), **mitigate** or **accept**.
